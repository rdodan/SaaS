Questions people ask

What does it cost?

The audit is free. After that every job has a fixed price agreed up front, so there are no surprises. Small fixes start small.

Can you remove my bad reviews?

No, and nobody honestly can unless a review breaks the platform's rules. What works is replying well and making it easy for your happy customers to leave a review, so the rating reflects your real work.

I already have a website. Is this still for me?

Yes. Often the site is fine and the gap is the Google profile, the reviews or how the site ranks. The audit shows where the real problem is.

How long does it take?

A Google profile tidy-up takes days. A new website usually takes two to three weeks. Search rankings improve over a few months.
