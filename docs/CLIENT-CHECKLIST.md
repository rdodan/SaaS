# New website: client checklist

Go through this with the client in one sitting (20 to 30 minutes). Anything they cannot
answer on the spot becomes a to-do with their name and a date next to it.

## 1. The goal

- [ ] What is the one thing a visitor should do? (call, book, request a quote, visit, order)
- [ ] Which services or products make you the most money? Which do you want more of?
- [ ] Who is your typical customer, and what do they usually ask before buying?
- [ ] Why do customers choose you over the others nearby?
- [ ] Which three competitors should I look at?
- [ ] Any websites you like the feel of? What exactly do you like about them?

## 2. Business details

- [ ] Exact business name, as it should appear everywhere
- [ ] Address, or the area covered if they travel to customers
- [ ] Phone number for the site. Can it receive texts?
- [ ] Email address for enquiries
- [ ] Opening hours, including holidays and seasonal changes
- [ ] Towns or areas served
- [ ] Registration, licence or insurance details that should be shown

## 3. Brand

- [ ] Logo file (original file, not a screenshot). If none: do they want one made?
- [ ] Brand colours, or the colour of the van, shopfront or uniform
- [ ] Which of the example styles do they prefer?
- [ ] Words that describe the business (friendly, premium, no-nonsense, traditional)

## 4. Content

- [ ] List of services, each with a one-line description
- [ ] Prices: exact, "from", or not shown?
- [ ] Menu, price list or class timetable, if relevant. Who updates it and how often?
- [ ] 10 to 20 real photos: the work, the premises, the team. Who took them?
- [ ] Before and after photos, if relevant
- [ ] A short "about us": how long in business, who runs it
- [ ] Staff to feature (names, roles, photos) and are they happy to appear?
- [ ] Questions customers always ask (these become the FAQ)
- [ ] Three or four reviews to quote, and has the reviewer agreed?

## 5. Features

- [ ] Contact: request form, live booking, or phone only?
- [ ] Do they already use a booking or ordering system?
- [ ] Should the chat bubble be on? What are the three most common questions?
- [ ] Where should enquiries be sent, and who answers them and how fast?
- [ ] Google reviews link, and a "leave a review" link
- [ ] Social media accounts to link
- [ ] Anything to sell or take payment for online?

## 6. Accounts and access

- [ ] Do they own a domain? Where was it bought, and who has the login?
- [ ] Existing website? Who hosts it, and can we get access?
- [ ] Is the Google Business Profile claimed? Add me as a manager.
- [ ] Email on the domain (e.g. info@theirbusiness): existing, wanted, or not needed?
- [ ] Any existing analytics or Search Console access

The client should own the domain, the Google profile and any paid accounts in their own
name, with you added as a manager. Never register them in yours.

## 7. Legal and permissions

- [ ] Confirm they own, or have permission to use, every photo, logo and piece of text
- [ ] Permission from anyone identifiable in photos or videos
- [ ] Privacy notice, and a cookie notice if analytics or embeds are used (rules vary by country)
- [ ] Any industry rules about what can be claimed or shown

## 8. The deal

- [ ] What is included: sections, features, number of rounds of changes (two is normal)
- [ ] Who supplies what, and by when
- [ ] Price, deposit (50% up front is normal) and when the rest is due
- [ ] Launch date, and what it depends on
- [ ] After launch: care plan (hosting, updates, small changes, monthly report) or one-off?
- [ ] Who signs off the final site?

## Before launch

- [ ] Client has read every page and approved it in writing
- [ ] Phone, email, address and hours checked against the Google profile, character for character
- [ ] Forms tested: the enquiry really arrives where it should
- [ ] Checked on a phone
- [ ] Domain pointed, site loads over https
- [ ] Google profile updated with the new website link
